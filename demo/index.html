Interactive playground

Try QoL on a safe fake account.

This playground now mirrors the real SpicyChat page shapes that QoL changes: Home, filters, Chats, Personas, My Creations, Lorebooks, chatbot and Lorebook editors, creator/profile pages and chat. The pages use safe local data while the QoL Options UI and feature registry come directly from the public extension source.

Experimental demo. This is still a work in progress and may be incomplete or break when the extension changes. I’m currently prioritizing work on the actual extension over improving the demo, so it may lag behind or stay a little rough for a while.
SFW demo only. The Home and creator surfaces use the SFW bots captured from my public @dragongraf1312 creator profile. Nothing logs in to SpicyChat, reads your account, sends messages, or calls private account APIs.
