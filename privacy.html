Privacy

Privacy policy.

The public policy covers the Chrome/Chromium and Firefox extensions plus the Android APK. QoL is designed around local-first storage and does not currently run a developer-controlled analytics, advertising, account or cloud-sync service.

Open full privacy policy

Local-first

Settings, saved lists, organization data, backups and most QoL-specific state stay in the user's browser/device whenever practical.

Optional integrations

Features that use an outside service are user-controlled. For example, DeepL translation sends selected message text to DeepL only when the user configures and uses that feature.

SpicyChat QoLindependent community project

SpicyChat has acknowledged the project, but SpicyChat QoL is still independent, unofficial, and not endorsed by SpicyChat.
