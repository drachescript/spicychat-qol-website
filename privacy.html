Privacy

Local-first by design.

Settings, lists, notes, Persona/bot copies, tracker data, backups and other QoL data stay in browser/extension storage whenever practical. Nothing is automatically sent to the developer.

Current privacy policyPermission explanations

Diagnostics

Support diagnostics are designed to avoid chat text, Memories, Persona text, private notes, API keys, webhook secrets, blocked-word lists and similar private content.

Optional outside services

Some features only contact an outside service when you explicitly configure/use them, such as DeepL translation, selected Wiki/Web Lorebook imports, user-supplied Discord webhooks or direct soundscape URLs.
